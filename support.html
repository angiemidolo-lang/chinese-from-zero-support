Common questions

  • Free lessons: Level 1 Lesson 1 and Lesson 2 are always free.
  • Subscription: Monthly and yearly plans unlock the full beginner path. Manage or cancel in Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Progress: Learning progress and language settings are stored only on this device.
  • Restore purchases: Use Restore purchases in the app Settings / Subscription screen.
  • Audio: Pronunciation uses on-device Apple speech synthesis. No microphone access is required.

简体中文帮助

如需帮助，请发邮件至 lj.app.support@gmail.com。

  • 免费课程：一级第 1、第 2 课永久免费。
