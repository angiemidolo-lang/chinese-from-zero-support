English简体中文SupportTerms of Use

English

Chinese From Zero is designed to work without an account. The current version does not collect, sell, or transmit personal information, advertising identifiers, or learning records to the developer or a third-party analytics server.

Data stored on your device

Learning progress, language settings, and related preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple system storage (UserDefaults).

Subscriptions

In-app subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide limited purchase receipts needed to unlock content on your device.

Device features

The app uses Apple on-device speech synthesis for pronunciation. It does not request access to your microphone, location, photos library, camera, or contacts. Lesson photos are bundled offline with the app.

Deleting data

Removing the app removes local progress stored by the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes

If app features or data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, email lj.app.support@gmail.com or use the support page.
